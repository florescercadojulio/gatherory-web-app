## Post

Life update! I got engaged so I built an app.

I watched my fiancé’s sister plan her wedding across four different group chats,
with different people and privacy needs. Keeping the ideas and decisions
together was a challenge.

So I built Gatherory: a place to plan with your people, share ideas, and decide
what you actually want to do.

Now they’re passing on what they learned to help us plan ours. That’s the idea
behind Blueprints: reusable event plans a community can share and adapt, so the
next person doesn’t have to start from scratch.

The iPhone beta is open! Give it a try and let me know what you think:
https://testflight.apple.com/join/rUDD4MCY
