# Gatherory marketing

Marketing strategy, positioning, acquisition experiments, and public-site briefs.

- [Launch strategy](launch-strategy.md): first audience, positioning, acquisition
  priorities, landing-page recommendations, four-week launch plan, and metrics.
- [Landing prototype handoff](../../../gatherory-landing/docs/launch-prototype.md):
  October 21 local hero/CTA implementation, English/Spanish iPhone pages,
  simulator assets, validation, and remaining launch work.

The strategy was recorded on October 21, 2026. It contains recommendations and
test hypotheses, not evidence of shipped features or approved campaign spending.
English is the initial campaign language; Spanish is supported through product
localization. Launch geography and marketing budget remain undecided.

Product evidence lives in the [product model](../architecture/product-model.md),
[release audit](../release/mobile-v1-guest-invitation-audit.md), and owning
roadmaps. Landing-page implementation belongs in the separate
`gatherory-landing` repository. Check current release availability before using
feature claims in public copy.
